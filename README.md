🌿 BloomBox – Smart Gardening & Plant Care

📖 About the Project

BloomBox is a gardening website designed for people who want to grow plants at home, on balconies, and in small gardens. It helps users discover and order seasonal plants, choose low-maintenance plants, and explore plant care and maintenance services.

Our goal is to make gardening simple, convenient, and enjoyable for everyone.

🎯 Objectives

- Promote home gardening and balcony planting.
- Help users select plants according to the season.
- Provide information about low-maintenance plants.
- Make plant ordering easy and convenient.
- Offer plant care and maintenance guidance.

✨ Features

- 🪴 Explore different plants and flowers.
- 🌱 Discover seasonal and low-maintenance plants.
- 🛒 Browse plants available for ordering.
- 💧 Learn about watering and plant care.
- 🌼 Explore balcony gardening ideas.
- 📱 Simple, attractive, and user-friendly design.

🛠️ Technologies Used

- HTML5 – Website structure.
- CSS3 – Styling and responsive layout.
- JavaScript – Interactive features.
- Backend & Database – To be integrated as the project develops.
